QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Does merging PDFs degrade vector lines or CAD blueprints?

No. CADConvert uses Apache PDFBox to perform lossless stream concatenation. Vector drawings, text layers, embedded fonts, and page orientations remain 100% untouched without any rasterization or degradation.

Can I reorder the PDF files before merging?

Yes. Use the up and down movement controls on each file card in the merge queue to arrange your documents in the exact order you want them combined.

Are uploaded PDF files stored permanently on disk?

Never. All documents are merged in volatile server RAM and streamed directly back to your browser. Files are purged immediately after the merge response completes.

Is there a limit on how many PDF files I can merge?

You can merge 2 or more PDF documents simultaneously up to the total upload buffer limit of 25 MB per request.
