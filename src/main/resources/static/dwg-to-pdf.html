QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between DWG and DXF?

DWG is Autodesk's native binary database format optimized for AutoCAD performance, while DXF is an open ASCII or binary exchange format. CADConvert seamlessly decodes binary DWG structures and outputs native vector PDF paths.

Does DWG to PDF conversion preserve vector geometry?

Yes. Every line, circle, arc, and dimension is compiled into mathematical PDF vector instructions using Apache PDFBox, allowing infinite zoom without blurriness or pixelation.

Are my DWG drawings kept private and secure?

Yes. CADConvert operates on an ephemeral in-memory policy. DWG files are converted in temporary volatile memory and immediately purged once the PDF stream is delivered. No drawing is ever stored or shared.

Do I need AutoCAD installed on my computer?

No. CADConvert runs entirely server-side with an open headless CAD bridge. You can convert AutoCAD DWG files directly in your web browser on Windows, Mac, Linux, iOS, or Android.
