QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

How does CADConvert compress PDF files without losing vector sharpness?

Our engine selectively targets high-resolution embedded raster photographs and underlays for downsampling and JPEG recompression, while leaving all mathematical vector line work, curves, and text completely lossless.

What are the three compression levels?

Mild (Low) retains maximum raster resolution for high-end plotting (~10–15% size reduction on embedded raster underlays); Recommended (Medium) downsamples images to 900px for general emailing and web display (~20–25% reduction); Extreme (High) aggressively compresses to 600px for strict municipal portals (~35–45% reduction). Pure vector geometry remains 100% untouched and lossless.

Is my PDF uploaded to permanent storage?

No. The optimization is executed 100% in volatile server memory (RAM) and immediately streamed back. No files or document metadata are saved to disk.

Can I compress architectural blueprints or CAD-generated PDFs?

Yes! The engine was specifically designed for architectural drawings and engineering documents, ensuring title blocks, thin lineweights, and text remain sharp.
